About CCOVFD

Neighbors protecting neighbors since 2001.

The Coryell City/Osage Volunteer Fire Department serves our rural community through fire suppression, rescue operations, emergency medical response, and public safety support.

Our department is made up of local men and women who volunteer their time, train year-round, and answer the call when their neighbors need them most.

Our History

Built by volunteers. Sustained by community.

CCOVFD was established to bring dependable emergency response to Coryell City, Osage, and the surrounding rural area. The mission is simple: protect life, protect property, and take care of one another.
