About CCOVFD

Local volunteers. Real commitment.

The Coryell City/Osage Volunteer Fire Department serves our rural community with dedication, courage, and neighborly care.

Our volunteers train year-round and respond to fires, rescues, medical emergencies, vehicle accidents, and other calls for help. In a small town, every call is personal.

Our History

Built here. Serving here.

CCOVFD was established to provide dependable emergency response to Coryell City, Osage, and nearby rural areas. Our equipment, training, and capabilities have grown, but the mission remains the same: take care of our neighbors.
