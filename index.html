About CCOVFD

Our Department

The Coryell City/Osage Volunteer Fire Department proudly serves our rural community with dedication, courage, and neighborly care.

Established in 2001, our department is made up of local men and women who volunteer their time to protect lives and property through fire suppression, rescue operations, medical emergency response, and public safety assistance.

In a small town, every call is personal. We are not only firefighters. We are your friends, family members, coworkers, and neighbors.

Through ongoing training, teamwork, and community involvement, we work hard to keep Coryell City, Osage, and the surrounding area safe and prepared.

Courage. Service. Community.

How We Serve

Emergency Services

🔥

Fire Response

Structure fires, grass fires, vehicle fires, and wildfire response throughout our rural service area.

🚒

Rescue Operations

Vehicle accidents, emergency extrication, hazardous situations, and assistance to neighboring departments.

✚

Medical Response

Emergency medical assistance and support until additional medical personnel arrive.

🏡

Community Support

Fire prevention education, safety demonstrations, public events, and community outreach.

Brush Truck

Rescue Unit

Carries rescue tools, medical supplies, and equipment for vehicle accidents and other emergencies.

Our Story

Serving Since 2001

The Coryell City/Osage Volunteer Fire Department was established in 2001 to provide dependable emergency services to our rural community.

Since then, generations of volunteers have answered the call, trained together, maintained equipment, and supported neighbors during some of their most difficult moments.

Our equipment and training continue to grow, but our mission remains the same: protect our community and take care of one another.
