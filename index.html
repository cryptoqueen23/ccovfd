About CCOVFD

Neighbors protecting neighbors since 2001.

The Coryell City/Osage Volunteer Fire Department serves our rural community through fire suppression, rescue operations, medical emergency response, and public safety support.

Our department is made up of local men and women who volunteer their time, train year-round, and answer the call when their neighbors need them.
